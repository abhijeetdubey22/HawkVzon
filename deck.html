💡 The Solution: HawkVzon HostelOS
Full-Stack Cloud Biometric Middleware & WhatsApp Parent-Safety Platform
  • Pure-Software Cloud Solution: Receives direct outbound HTTP POST punch events from existing Rs 12,000 eSSL and Matrix turnstiles (via ADMS protocol). Zero local PC servers or relays required on premises.
  • 1-Ping/Day Safe Return WhatsApp: Dispatches an automated evening check-in alert to parents between 05:00 PM – 09:30 PM with a strict 6-hr cooldown (capping Meta charges at Rs 0.115/msg).
  • Parent-Approved Digital Out-Pass: Students request weekend leaves on mobile; parents approve via interactive WhatsApp buttons; turnstiles only unlatch for approved passes.
  • Night Curfew Radar & Gemini Intelligence: Live warden console reconciles attendance at 09:30 PM. Google Gemini 1.5 Flash generates daily executive safety briefings.
🚀 Why It Wins in Student Coaching Capitals
High-Retention Emotional Safety Loop + Capital-Light Unit Economics
  • Instant 30-Minute Remote Onboarding: Enter our cloud IP & port on the existing turnstile keypad. Zero technician site visits or wiring required.
  • 94% Parent Engagement on WhatsApp: Parents actively read arrival notifications daily; replaces unread 4% open-rate SMS.
  • 88% Software Gross Margins: Priced at Rs 30/bed/month (Rs 43,200/year for 120 beds) against direct COGS of only Rs 5,118/year.
  • 100% DPDP Act 2023 Compliant: Zero raw photos stored in the cloud. Only one-way 128-dimensional Fernet AES-256 encrypted biometric tokens.
